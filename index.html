Our Core Approach

We believe that the future of healthcare lies in understanding disease before it occurs, preventing illness whenever possible, and delivering interventions informed by the genetic, environmental, clinical, and social characteristics of populations.

Core Flagship Programmes

GPMAI

Genomics & Precision Medicine Awareness Initiative: Promoting public understanding through community outreach and school engagement.

CGDSP

Community Genomic & Disease Screening: Free genomic screening across priority conditions including Sickle Cell Disease and Cancer.

GRIP

Genomics Research & Innovation Programme: Advancing multidisciplinary genomics research grounded in locally generated data.

HPAP

Health Policy & Advocacy Programme: Generating evidence and technical recommendations to support health governance.
