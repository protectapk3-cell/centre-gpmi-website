Ways to Partner With Us

There are several ways to work with Centre-GPMI — choose the path that fits your organization, or reach out and we'll help you find the right fit.

💰 Funding Partners

Support our pilot programmes and research initiatives, from community screening campaigns to long-term capacity-building efforts.

🔬 Research Collaborators

Partner with us on genomics and precision medicine research relevant to African populations, including data-sharing and co-investigation.

🏥 Laboratory & Clinical Partners

Join our network of accredited reference laboratories and healthcare providers supporting screening, diagnosis, and referral pathways.

🏛️ Institutional & Government Partners

Collaborate with us on policy, regulatory alignment, and public health system integration.

🤝 Community & Civil Society Partners

Help us reach communities on the ground with genomic literacy campaigns and screening programmes.
