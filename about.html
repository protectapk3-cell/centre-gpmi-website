About Centre-GPMI

Our Origin & Mission

The Centre for Genomics and Precision Medicine Initiatives (Centre-GPMI) is a registered non-governmental organization established to pioneer cutting-edge genomic research and clinical precision medicine applications within Nigeria and across the broader African continent [1].


Our core focus is to address the severe underrepresentation of African genetic data in global healthcare studies, creating personalized medical frameworks that directly improve clinical outcomes for local populations.

Our Vision

To be the leading hub for genomic innovation in Africa, where precision medicine transforms routine patient care and eradicates health inequalities.

Our Strategy

By building strategic partnerships with international researchers, training local data scientists, and advising public health policymakers on genetic medicine infrastructure.
