About Centre-GPMI

Organizational Overview

The Centre for Health Genomics and Precision Medicine Initiatives (Centre-GPMI) is an independent, non-profit, non-partisan, non-governmental organization dedicated to advancing genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health within healthcare systems, research, policy, and community development.

Our Vision

A future in which genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health transform healthcare systems, enabling every individual and community to achieve predictive, personalized, and equitable care.

Our Mission

To advance the responsible integration of genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health into healthcare systems through research, innovation, education, and community engagement.

Thematic Areas of Focus

  • Genomics and Genomic Research
  • Precision Medicine and Precision Public Health
  • Health Systems Strengthening
  • Disease Prevention and Control
  • Health Policy and Governance
  • Molecular Epidemiology & Genomic Surveillance
