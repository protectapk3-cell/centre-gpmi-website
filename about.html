About Centre-GPMI

Organizational Overview

The Centre for Health Genomics and Precision Medicine Initiatives (Centre-GPMI) is an independent, non-profit, non-partisan, non-governmental organization dedicated to advancing genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health within healthcare systems, research, policy, and community development. We operate at the intersection of biomedical science, public health, and technology.

Our Vision

A future in which genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health transform healthcare systems, enabling every individual and community to achieve the highest attainable standard of health through predictive, preventive, personalised, and equitable care.

Our Mission

To advance the responsible integration of genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health into healthcare systems through research, innovation, education, policy development, institutional strengthening, and community engagement.

Our Core Values

Our identity and actions are guided by: Scientific Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Equity, Collaboration, Community-Centred Service, Respect for Human Dignity, Accountability, Professionalism, and Sustainability.

Thematic Areas of Focus

  • Genomics and Genomic Research
  • Precision Medicine and Precision Public Health
  • Health Systems Strengthening
  • Disease Prevention and Control
  • Health Policy and Governance
  • Molecular Epidemiology & Genomic Surveillance
  • Bioinformatics and Digital Health
  • Biosecurity, Biosafety & Emergency Preparedness
  • Capacity Building & Workforce Development
  • Health Education & Community Engagement
