Who We Are

Our Story

The Centre for Health Genomics and Precision Medicine Initiatives (Centre-GPMI) is an independent, non-profit, non-partisan, non-governmental organization dedicated to advancing genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health within healthcare systems, research, policy, and community development. Headquartered in Jos, Plateau State, Centre-GPMI was established to close a critical gap: while genomic medicine is reshaping healthcare worldwide, most African health systems — including Nigeria's — have limited capacity to research, translate, and deliver its benefits locally.

Centre-GPMI is building that capacity from the ground up — strong governance, a skilled workforce, ethical research infrastructure, and community-facing programmes designed for real-world impact.

Our Vision

A future in which genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health transform healthcare systems, enabling every individual and community to achieve predictive, personalized, and equitable care.

Our Mission

To advance the responsible integration of genomics, precision medicine, and evidence-based public health into healthcare systems through research, innovation, education, and community engagement.

Our Values

  • Scientific Rigor — Every programme is grounded in sound science and ethical research practice.
  • Equity — Genomic medicine should serve everyone, not just those with the means to access it.
  • Integrity — We hold ourselves accountable to the communities and partners we serve.
  • Collaboration — Lasting impact requires partnership across research, government, and community.
  • Local Ownership — Building African capacity and African-led solutions, not dependency.

Governance

Centre-GPMI is governed by a Board of Trustees responsible for fiduciary oversight and strategic direction, supported by a Scientific Advisory Board that provides technical guidance on research quality and direction. The Centre is legally registered in Nigeria as an Incorporated Trustees organization, with full regulatory compliance including tax registration.
