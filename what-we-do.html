Our Flagship Programmes

Genomics & Precision Medicine Awareness Initiative (GPMAI) +

This national framework drives community sensitization and education. We run high school outreach projects, translate technical genomics information into accessible local languages, and run public workshops to debunk genetic myths across Nigeria.

Community Genomic & Disease Screening Programme (CGDSP) +

Direct molecular testing interventions targeting vulnerable populations. This framework provides screening data access channels for major health concerns including Sickle Cell Disease (SCD), specific hereditary cancers, and common metabolic health disorders.

Genomics Research & Innovation Programme (GRIP) +

The institutional engine supporting robust biomedical analysis. GRIP works to construct structured clinical sample biobanks, train data technicians in complex bioinformatics processing algorithms, and produce insights utilizing locally sourced African genomes.

Health Policy & Advocacy Programme (HPAP) +

Bridging the gap between scientific evidence and state guidance. HPAP translates molecular data metrics into actionable policy briefs to advise the Federal Ministry of Health and create institutional ethical safety blueprints.
